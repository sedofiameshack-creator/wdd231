  • Free membership for registered non-profit organizations

  • Discounted event registration
  • Quarterly promotion in the newsletter

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Premium directory listing with featured placement
  • Home page member spotlight rotation
  • Unlimited training workshops
  • Event sponsorship opportunities
  • Free admission to the Annual General Meeting
